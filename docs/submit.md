# Submit your app

Built something with [jaaicode](https://jaaicode.org)? Add it here.

Open an issue or PR on the
[site repo](https://github.com/tomz/jaaiworks-site) with:

- **Name** of the app
- **One-line description** (≤ 100 chars)
- **Link** to a public site, repo, or demo
- *(Optional)* a 1024×640 PNG screenshot
- *(Optional)* a one-paragraph "how jaaicode helped" note

Submissions are reviewed weekly and merged in batches.
